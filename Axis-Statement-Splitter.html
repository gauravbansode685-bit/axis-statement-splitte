Axis Statement Splitter

Add the monthly Bank Transaction Status workbook. The tool reads the 7036 and 6055 sheets and builds two import files: all credits from 7036, and the 6055 credits marked “Awaiting HMG Collection Report”.

Dates are read as dd/mm/yyyy and written as real Excel dates, so they don't flip day and month. For cheque (CLG) rows the UTR column is filled as “Cheque no- …”, and for NEFT, RTGS, IMPS and UPI rows with the reference from Particulars. Customer, project, address and plant columns are left blank to fill from the collection report.
